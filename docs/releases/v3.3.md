## v3.3 - Rate and channels as settings: 250 Hz × 16 by default, CH9-CH16 as buttons

ExpressLRS is now asked for **250 Hz and 16 channels** (3.2: always 100 Hz and 8 channels), and both are settings you can change while the module streams. **CH9-CH16 are joystick buttons 1-8**, so your switches reach the sim too.

### What was happening

3.2 asked every module for the same thing, fixed in the code: `action=joystick_begin&interval=10000&channels=8`. ExpressLRS streams what it is asked for, so an ELRS radio gave the sim a new stick position only every 10 ms, and CH9-CH16 never arrived. Crossfire ignores the request and always sent 16 channels, but the app used only the first 8.

### The fix

The activation asks for a rate and a channel count ("the request"). Defaults: **250 Hz** (4000 µs) and **16 channels**.

| Rate | 1000 Hz | 500 Hz | 333 Hz | **250 Hz** | 200 Hz | 150 Hz | 100 Hz | 50 Hz |
|------|---------|--------|--------|------------|--------|--------|--------|-------|
| Interval (µs) | 1000 | 2000 | 3000 | **4000** | 5000 | 6667 | 10000 | 20000 |

Channels: **4 to 16**.

- **GUI**: a new row under Port and Module IP with two drop-downs, **Rate** and **Channels**. A change applies at once.
- **Command line** (GUI and CLI): `--rate HZ` (any 10 to 1000), `--interval US` (1000 to 100000), `--channels N` (4 to 16), also as `--rate=500`. Example: `ELRSWifiJoystick.exe --rate 500 --channels 8`. The GUI shows a mistake (a value that is not supported, a missing value, an unknown option, a module IP that is not `a.b.c.d`) in a message box that says what it uses instead. The CLI prints it with the usage and exits.
- **CLI keys**: `r` rate, `c` channels, `a` restart the stream. On redirected stdin: `rate <hz>`, `interval <us>`, `channels <n>`, `restart`, and also `start`, `stop`, `status`.
- **Stats** show what arrives next to what was asked: `Rate: 249 Hz (asked 250)`, and `8 ch (asked 16)` when the channel count differs.

A higher rate means lower latency, a bit more WiFi traffic and CPU. 250 Hz with 16 channels is about 16 KB/s and suits most sims.

### How a change reaches the module

- **ExpressLRS** (3.4.0 and later: the first with the WiFi joystick) takes a new request only while it is not streaming. So a change while it streams **restarts its stream**: `joystick_end`, then `joystick_begin` with the new values (`Restarting the stream of <ip>: 500 Hz, 16 channels`). The joystick holds its position for that moment and the lock stays.
- **A restart that fails** (no 2xx answer) is sent again 5 s after its last send while the module still streams to the app, 3 sends in all: then the log says the app gave up. A module that stops streaming meanwhile, or whose restart gave up, is restarted once more when it streams to the app again.
- **A module of unknown kind** that streams 16 channels and has sent no beacon may be Crossfire. Its restart waits until 20 s after the app locked on to it, and is dropped if a Crossfire beacon comes first.
- **A change while the module does not stream to the app** (WiFi dropout, Stop) goes into its next `joystick_begin`. The app remembers what it asked each module, also across Stop and Start.
- **A module still streaming from before** the app started keeps its old rate (the frames don't carry it): pick another rate and back, or press `a` in the CLI. A different channel count is fixed by itself.

All the rules: [Rate & channels](https://github.com/juricabi/ELRS-Crossfire-WiFi-Joystick-Windows#-rate--channels) in the README.

### Buttons: CH9-CH16

| Channel | vJoy |
|---------|------|
| CH1-CH8 | The 8 axes, same order as 3.2: X, Y, RX, RY, RZ, Z, Slider 0, Slider 1 |
| CH9-CH16 | Buttons 1-8: pressed at 75 % (raw ≥ 24576), released below 62.5 % (raw < 20480) |

- The gap between the two thresholds is hysteresis: a switch sitting near one never flickers.
- The vJoy device needs **8 buttons** (the default: check in *Configure vJoy*, then restart the app). The log says how many it has.
- All 8 buttons need 16 channels. With Channels at 8 the app says `Buttons (CH9-CH16): none - the module sends 8 channels`.
- The GUI shows them in a new row under the axis bars, the CLI in its status line (`B:1--4----`).
- The axis order did not change, so your sim bindings carry over.

### Also fixed

- **The joystick centers when the stream stops.** 3.2 kept the last axis values when no data came for 3 s (radio off, out of range). On start, Stop and exit it reset vJoy, which put RX, RY, RZ and the sliders at their minimum (throttle at zero, yaw at full deflection). Now the app starts with the 8 axes at the center, and lock loss, Stop, closing the app and Connect to another module put them back there and release the buttons. That includes the throttle (CH3, RX): the sim sees 50 %. Shorter gaps still hold the last values.
- **Connect** asks the new module at once (`connecting to <ip>`). In 3.2, Connect while another module kept streaming to the PC could send the new module nothing: the activation and the lock check ran only after 100 ms without any packet. They now run at least 10 times a second, whatever arrives.
- **Connect to the module that streams now** keeps it: nothing is sent, nothing centered.
- **Module IPs are checked** (`a.b.c.d`). A typo is reported instead of being used.
- **vJoy write errors** (device changed in *Configure vJoy*, driver restarted) are logged once, and again when the writes get through.
- **CLI**: the status line shows the asked rate and the buttons, and fits an 80-column console.

### Upgrading from 3.2

- The app saves no settings (3.2 neither), so there is nothing to carry over: replace the exe and run it.
- Rate and Channels start at 250 Hz and 16 on every launch. For other start values, add them to your shortcut. `--rate 100 --channels 8` gives the 3.2 values.
- The firewall rule from 3.2 (`ELRS WiFi Joystick`, UDP 11000) still covers the app: no new UAC prompt.

### 🛑 Crossfire users

Crossfire / Tracer starts on the same request but **ignores both values**: it always sends 16 channels at about 90 Hz. While connected to one, the Rate and Channels drop-downs are greyed out (`Crossfire ignores these: always 16 channels at about 90 Hz`). A module known as Crossfire by its beacon is never sent `joystick_end`. New for you: CH9-CH16 work as buttons.

The app knows Crossfire by its `VELOCIDRONE` beacon (about every 8 s): until the first one arrives, the drop-downs stay enabled. If its beacons never reach the PC (broadcasts blocked, the module set by IP), it is taken for ELRS after 20 s, and a Rate or Channels change then sends it `joystick_end`: leave them at their defaults.

Some TBS firmware versions still break the WiFi joystick **completely**: the module streams, but no stick data ever arrives, in any client (including VelociDrone Mobile):

| Component | Works | Broken |
|-----------|-------|--------|
| **Crossfire TX (XF)** | **6.31**, **6.36** | 6.42 public, 6.48 beta, 6.48 public |
| **WiFi module** | **v2.17** up to **v3.10** (v2.25 recommended) | v3.20 |

Confirmed by the VelociDrone developers: [TBS firmware breaks VelociDrone mobile wi-fi support](https://batcavegames.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/16000225491).

### Requirements
- Windows 7 SP1 to Windows 11 (64-bit), no .NET install needed (self-contained)
- vJoy driver: 2.1.9.1 ([SourceForge](http://vjoystick.sourceforge.net/)) or 2.2.x ([BrunnerInnovation](https://github.com/BrunnerInnovation/vJoy/releases)), with 8 buttons for CH9-CH16
- ELRS TX module 3.4.0 or later (the first with the WiFi joystick), or TBS Crossfire/Tracer: see the firmware table above
