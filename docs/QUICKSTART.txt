ELRS / TBS Crossfire WiFi Joystick for Windows  -  v3.3
=========================================================

Use your ExpressLRS or TBS Crossfire/Tracer radio as a wireless joystick on
Windows (VelociDrone, Liftoff, DRL, ...) - no cables.

SETUP
-----
1. Install vJoy (once):   http://vjoystick.sourceforge.net/  (2.1.9.1)
      or 2.2.x from       https://github.com/BrunnerInnovation/vJoy/releases
2. Run  ELRSWifiJoystick.exe
   - Accept the one-time Windows permission (UAC) prompt - this adds the
     firewall rule so the joystick data can get through.
3. Put your ELRS / Crossfire module on the SAME WiFi network as this PC.
   CROSSFIRE FIRMWARE - some TBS versions break this feature completely
   (no stick data reaches ANY app, including VelociDrone Mobile):
       Crossfire TX : use 6.31 or 6.36   - BROKEN on 6.42 and 6.48
       WiFi module  : use v2.17 to v3.10 - BROKEN on v3.20  (v2.25 recommended)
   - The banner turns GREEN "Connected - streaming" and the axis bars move.
   - Or type the module's IP into "Module IP" and click Connect (Stop clears
     it: the next Start auto-discovers).
4. In your simulator, select "vJoy Device" as the controller and calibrate.
   CH1-CH8 are the vJoy axes, CH9-CH16 joystick buttons 1-8 (a switch is
   pressed above 75%). The vJoy device needs 8 buttons (Configure vJoy).

RATE & CHANNELS
---------------
The Rate and Channels boxes say what the module is asked for: 250 Hz and
16 channels by default (1000, 500, 333, 250, 200, 150, 100 or 50 Hz;
4 to 16 channels).
- ExpressLRS (3.4.0 and later) takes a change by restarting its stream: at
  once, or within 20 s for a module that has sent no beacon yet and streams
  16 channels (it may be a Crossfire) - also after a WiFi dropout, when the
  module is back.
- A change while the module is not connected (WiFi dropout, Stop) goes into
  its next start, and reaches a module that kept streaming when it is back:
  the app remembers what it asked each module, also across Stop and Start.
- A restart the module does not take (no OK to its stop and start) is sent
  again 5 s later, 3 sends in all (the first and up to 2 more); then the log
  says the app gave up. After a WiFi dropout or Stop, or once the app gave
  up, it goes out once more when the module streams again.
- Crossfire / Tracer ignores both - always 16 channels at about 90 Hz - so
  the boxes are greyed out while it is connected. A module known as
  Crossfire by its beacon is never sent a stop.
- Connect to the module that streams now changes nothing; Connect to another
  one centers the joystick and asks the new one at once.
- Start values from the command line, e.g.:
      ELRSWifiJoystick.exe --rate 500 --channels 8

TIPS
----
- Windows SmartScreen may warn about an unknown publisher (the app is not
  code-signed). Click "More info" -> "Run anyway".
- Click "Help" in the app for short tutorials on every feature.
- Minimize to the system tray while flying - the joystick keeps working and
  CPU use drops to almost nothing.
- The module keeps broadcasting until it is powered off; that's normal
  (closing the app only stops reading it).
- When no data comes for 3 s (radio off, out of range), the vJoy axes go to
  the center and the buttons are released; the same on Stop, on closing and
  on Connect to another module. Shorter gaps hold the last values.
